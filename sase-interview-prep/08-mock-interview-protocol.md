# Mock Interview Protocol

**To start:** tell Claude *"Start mock interview mode"* (optionally: *"technical only"*, *"behavioral only"*, or *"full loop"*).

## Rules
- One question at a time. No answers in advance.
- Answer as you would in the room: spoken-style, timed if you like.
- After each answer you get:
  1. **Scores (1–5)** for each category below
  2. **What was missing** (specific technical gaps or vague claims)
  3. **A follow-up challenge**, the way a real Palo Alto interviewer would probe
  4. **An improved answer**, grounded in your actual Cisco/Juniper experience (no invented accomplishments)
- Say "skip" to move on, "harder" to escalate, or "debrief" for a summary of patterns and the top 3 fixes.

## Scoring rubric
| Category | 1 (weak) | 3 (solid) | 5 (exceptional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical accuracy** | Wrong or confused terminology | Correct, minor gaps | Precise, includes the edge cases and verified caveats |
| **Architecture depth** | Feature listing | Explains mechanism and flow | Mechanism + failure modes + scale/HA considerations |
| **Problem-solving approach** | Jumps to a conclusion | Logical sequence | Hypothesis-driven, evidence at each step, prevention |
| **Clear communication** | Rambling, jargon | Structured | Structured, concise, adapts to the audience, uses a whiteboard mentally |
| **Strategic judgment** | No tradeoffs | Names tradeoffs | Decides, defends, and says what would change the decision |
| **Role relevance** | Pure engineer *or* pure trainer | Connects to enablement | Turns the technical insight into a scalable field readiness move |

## Planned question bank (hidden from you during the mock; Claude draws from it)
